Never lose your place in a spreadsheet again.

CellGlide highlights the active row and column, glides between cells with smooth animation, and brings Excel-style selection to Google Sheets™. Runs entirely in your browser and never changes your data.

Add to ChromeFree for 30 days

Focus highlight

Soft bands on the active row and column so your eye lands on the right cell in wide sheets. Row only, column only, or both; pick a color and intensity.

Smooth cell animation

The selection glides between cells on keyboard and mouse instead of jumping. Respects your system's Reduce Motion setting.

Excel-style selection

Green outline and gray range shading, the way Excel does it, so switching between the two feels natural.

Privacy, in one paragraph

CellGlide reads only the position of the selection, grid and scrollbars on docs.google.com/spreadsheets pages so it can draw its overlay. It does not read cell contents, formulas or your Google account, changes nothing in your spreadsheet, and makes no network requests other than checking your license with ExtensionPay.

CellGlide

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

CellGlide is a Chrome extension that highlights and animates the selected cell in Google Sheets™. It runs entirely in your browser.

What it reads. On `docs.google.com/spreadsheets` pages the extension measures the position of the selection outline, grid clipping and scrollbars so it can draw its overlay. It does not read cell text, formulas, the formula bar, clipboard contents, or your Google account.

What it changes. Nothing in your spreadsheet. It adds its own visual layer and stylesheet only. Collaborators never see it.

What it stores. Display preferences and the spreadsheet IDs they belong to, kept in Chrome's extension storage on your device (and in your Chrome sync profile if sync is on). These never leave your browser.

Payments and license. Payments are handled by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) and Stripe. To check whether a free trial or purchase is active, the extension exchanges an anonymous, randomly generated key with extensionpay.com; your email is provided only by you, to ExtensionPay, when you choose to continue the free trial or to buy (and to Stripe at checkout). CellGlide itself receives no card details and does not transmit anything about your spreadsheets. See ExtensionPay's policy at https://extensionpay.com/privacy and Stripe's at https://stripe.com/privacy.

No analytics, no ads, no sale of data. The extension makes no network requests other than the license check described above.
